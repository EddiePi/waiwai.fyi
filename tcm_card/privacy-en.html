Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 25, 2026

TCM Card / 本草卡 (“the App”) respects your privacy. This policy explains what we collect, how we use it, and the choices you have.

1. Information We Collect

  • Study data (stored on your device): the collections you build, your study progress, “Got It / Have Question” marks, and quiz records are stored on your device and are not uploaded to our servers.
  • Anonymous identifier: the App uses Firebase anonymous sign-in to generate a random anonymous identifier. It is not linked to your real identity, name, email, or Apple ID.
  • Content-correction reports: when you report a card as incorrect, we receive the card ID, card version, the reason you selected, an optional note, the anonymous identifier, the app version, and a timestamp.
  • Usage & diagnostics: we collect anonymous usage statistics (such as feature usage and stability) via Firebase Analytics to improve the product.

2. How We Use Information

  • To provide, maintain, and improve the App;
  • To correct card content based on your feedback;
  • To analyze overall usage and stability.

We do not sell your personal information.

3. Sharing of Information

We use Google Firebase (Authentication, Firestore, and Analytics) as our backend. That data is processed by Google under its privacy policy. We do not disclose your information to other third parties except as required by law or to protect legitimate interests.

4. Data Retention

On-device data is retained with the App and cleared when you delete it. Correction reports and analytics are retained for as long as needed to fulfill the purposes above.

5. Security

We take reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect data. However, no method of transmission over the Internet or electronic storage is completely secure.

6. Children’s Privacy

The App is intended for adult learners. It is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their personal information.

7. Your Choices and Rights

You can stop using and delete the App at any time to clear on-device data. To request assistance or exercise rights available to you under applicable law, contact us at the email below.

8. Third-Party Services

The App uses Google Firebase (Authentication, Firestore, Analytics). Its data processing is subject to the Google / Firebase Privacy Policy.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. For material changes, we will update the “Last updated” date at the top of this page.

10. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, contact us at support@waiwai.fyi.
