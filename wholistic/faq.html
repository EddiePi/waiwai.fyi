Wholistic — Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about building recurring checklists, sharing lists, alerts, pricing, and device support.

What is Wholistic?

Wholistic is an iPhone app for building checklists for things you do again and again. You create a template once — for a weekly review, trip packing, or home maintenance — then launch a fresh checklist from it whenever you need it, share it with others, set alerts, and track everything in a calendar view.

How do templates work in Wholistic?

A template is a reusable set of tasks you define once for a recurring event. Tapping a template generates a new, ready-to-use checklist with every item reset to unchecked, so you never have to rebuild the same list from scratch.

Can I share a checklist with other people?

Yes. You can invite others onto any list or template using a share code or link. Depending on how you configure sharing, collaborators can check off items together in real time, or receive a read-only copy to use on their own.

Does Wholistic send reminders or alerts?

Yes. You can attach alerts to an entire event or to individual checklist items, so reminders arrive for specific tasks rather than one generic notification for the whole list.

Is Wholistic free to download?

Yes, Wholistic is free to download on the App Store. An optional premium tier, Wholistic Plus, unlocks additional features through in-app purchase.

How much does Wholistic Plus cost?

Wholistic Plus is available as a monthly subscription ($2.99, with a 1-week free trial), an annual subscription ($23.99, with a 1-week free trial), or a one-time lifetime purchase ($44.99). Prices may vary by region and are billed through the Apple App Store.

What devices and iOS version does Wholistic require?

Wholistic is built for iPhone and requires iOS 17.2 or later. It is not currently available for iPad, Mac, or Android.

What languages does Wholistic support?

Wholistic supports English, Simplified Chinese, Spanish, and Traditional Chinese.

Do I need to create an account to use Wholistic?

No account is required. Wholistic assigns your device a random anonymous identifier to enable syncing and collaboration without sign-up.
